# Accountable review pack

Use one signed copy per reviewer. Do not publicly name a reviewer until scope, payment, conflicts and permission to be named are recorded.

## Required decision

For each page or claim, mark one:

- approved within stated scope;
- approved with required changes;
- requires another discipline;
- not approved for publication.

## APD review scope

- ADHD, eating, appetite, nutrition and interoception claims;
- medication-related appetite content;
- eating-disorder risk and escalation language;
- weight-neutral and non-restrictive framing;
- boundaries between AMHSW therapy and individual dietetic advice.

## Medical review scope

- assessment and differential-diagnosis content;
- medication, monitoring and physical-health content;
- prescriber/GP/psychiatrist roles;
- red flags and escalation points;
- national statements that may vary by jurisdiction.

## First Nations governance scope

- cultural claims and representation;
- distinction between Aboriginal-led practice and person-defined cultural safety;
- community benefit, accountability and feedback;
- data, privacy and use of identity;
- whether broader public claims exceed the founder's individual authority.
